RATING SYSTEM · VERSION 1

How Elo Works

Q2Stats uses separate Elo ratings for Team Games and 1v1. Every player starts at 1500 Elo in each category. Here is how the current algorithm calculates rating changes.

1. Team Games: Match Result & Team Strength

The system calculates the average Elo of each team and estimates the probability of winning. The result is used to determine a base Elo change.

Expected = 1 / (1 + 10^((OpponentTeamAverageElo - YourTeamAverageElo) / 400)) BaseElo = 16 * (Result - Expected)

Result = 1 for a win, 0 for a loss. The team K-factor is 16. Beating a stronger team produces a larger base gain; losing to a stronger team produces a smaller base loss.

2. Individual Performance (Perf)

In Team Games, the base Elo change is adjusted using each player's individual performance.

MetricWeightRaw statistic
Net40%Frags − Deaths
K/D25%Frags / Deaths*
Net Damage25%Damage Dealt − Damage Received
Discipline10%Suicides, teamkills and team damage

Net, K/D and Net Damage are converted to percentile scores by comparing each player with all players in the match, including teammates and opponents. A percentile counts the share of other players with a strictly lower value; ties do not count as lower.

Percentile = 100 * (NumberOfOtherPlayersWithLowerValue / (TotalPlayers - 1)) Perf = (NetPercentile * 0.40) + (KDPercentile * 0.25) + (DamagePercentile * 0.25) + (Discipline * 0.10) Discipline = clamp(100 - 15*Suicides - 10*Teamkills - 0.02*TeamDamage, 0, 100)

* If deaths are zero, the current algorithm uses frags as the K/D value instead of dividing by zero. For a single-player percentile input, the percentile is 100.

3. Final Team Elo Change

WIN: EloChange = clamp(BaseElo + (Perf - 50) * 0.25, 0, 16) LOSS: EloChange = clamp(BaseElo + (Perf - 50) * 0.34 + CloseLossBonus, -16, 7)

Close-loss bonus: +1 if the player loses, has Perf of at least 80, and the score difference is 5 or less.

A player can gain up to +7 Elo on a loss after a strong individual performance. A winning player can receive 0 Elo after a weak performance.

5. 1v1 Elo

1v1 uses standard win/loss Elo without individual performance adjustments. Opponent strength affects the expected result.

Expected = 1 / (1 + 10^((OpponentElo - YourElo) / 400)) EloChange = 32 * (Result - Expected)

Starting Elo is 1500, and the 1v1 K-factor is 32. Frags, deaths, Net, K/D and damage do not directly affect 1v1 Elo.

6. Development & Feedback

This is the current v1 algorithm, not a finished or perfect measure of skill. In particular, Net and K/D have a strong influence on the individual performance adjustment. As more matches are played, we want to evaluate the results and fine-tune the balance between winning and individual contribution.

Feedback from the Quake 2 community is welcome.
